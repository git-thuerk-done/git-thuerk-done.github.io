## Overview

The [Datadog Administrator's Guide](https://docs.datadoghq.com/administrators_guide/) provides administrators with a centralized starting point for planning, implementing, and managing Datadog within their organizations.

Organized around three key stages—**Plan, Build, and Run**—the guide helps administrators navigate implementation considerations, recommended practices, and ongoing operational responsibilities.

## The Challenge

Datadog administrators needed a clear entry point to understand how to approach platform administration and locate relevant guidance and best practices.

The challenge was to organize technical information into a cohesive resource that supported administrators throughout their implementation and operational workflows.

## My Role & Contributions

I led the documentation initiative, establishing the guide's information architecture and collaborating with Sales Engineers, Product teams, and Product Managers to develop and deliver the content.

My contributions included:

- **Information architecture:** Designed the Plan, Build, and Run framework to organize documentation around administrator workflows and the platform lifecycle.
- **Content development:** Collaborated with stakeholders to identify key topics, establish content requirements, and develop clear, actionable guidance for administrators.
- **Technical writing:** Authored and refined documentation to ensure clarity, consistency, and alignment with administrator needs.
- **Editorial coordination:** Led content reviews, incorporated stakeholder feedback, and coordinated publication of the guide.

## Outcome

- Delivered a centralized, structured documentation resource that gives Datadog administrators a clear starting point for navigating platform administration and recommended practices.

- The guide brings together relevant technical guidance within an organized framework, making it easier for administrators to identify information appropriate to their implementation and operational needs.

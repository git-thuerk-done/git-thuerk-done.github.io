## Overview

[How Step Duration is Determined in Browser Tests](https://docs.datadoghq.com/synthetics/guide/step-duration/) explains how Datadog Synthetic Monitoring calculates the duration of individual Browser Test steps, including page loading, network activity, and execution timeouts.

The guide helps customers understand unexpected step durations and troubleshoot test performance.

## The Challenge

Customers were repeatedly submitting feature requests related to Browser Test step duration.

During a Synthetics Engineering summit, I participated in discussions focused on identifying high-impact customer pain points and potential product improvements.

Further investigation with Support and Engineering revealed that the underlying challenge could be addressed through clearer technical guidance rather than a change to the product's code.

## My Role & Contributions

I identified and developed a documentation-based solution to a recurring customer challenge, working closely with Synthetics Engineering and Support to understand the technical behavior and customer needs.

My contributions included:

- **Customer advocacy:** Participated in the Synthetics Engineering summit to evaluate recurring, high-impact customer feature requests and identify opportunities for improvement.
- **Technical investigation:** Collaborated with Engineering and Support to understand Browser Test step duration behavior and determine the underlying source of customer confusion.
- **Solution identification:** Helped establish that the customer need could be addressed through targeted documentation rather than a product code change.
- **Technical writing:** Independently authored the guide, incorporating technical expertise and feedback from Support to explain step duration behavior and provide actionable troubleshooting guidance.

## Outcome

- Authored and published a targeted technical guide addressing a recurring customer need that had previously surfaced through feature requests.

- By translating complex product behavior into actionable documentation, the guide provides customers with a clearer understanding of Browser Test step duration and a resource for troubleshooting unexpected results.

- The initiative demonstrated how documentation can serve as an effective solution to customer challenges without requiring changes to product functionality.

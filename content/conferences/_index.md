## LavaCon 2026

I attended LavaCon 2026, a conference focused on content strategy, technical communication, and the evolving role of content professionals.

The conference was an opportunity to connect with others in the field, explore new approaches to content strategy, and learn how teams are using AI, collaboration, and emerging technologies to improve content experiences.

One of my biggest takeaways was the importance of looking beyond individual documentation deliverables and thinking more strategically about how content connects people, processes, and technology.

I'm excited to apply these perspectives to my work and continue exploring how technical documentation can create meaningful value for customers and organizations.
